Make a theme

    Propose to the store

    Try it in Discord

    1. You need Sunbird 0.1.1 or newer and its desktop-themes plugin, installed from Sunbird's plugin catalog in Discord's settings (listed under Unverified for now).
    2. Download your theme, then in desktop-themes press Open themes folder, put the file there and reload the plugins.
    3. Pick it under Theme.

    What proposing does

    GitHub opens with your file ready to add. It makes a copy of the store under your account, then you open a pull request. Once your theme passes the checks and is reviewed, it shows up on this site and in everyone's Ultracord.
