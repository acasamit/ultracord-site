Recolor your Discord

Ultracord is a community hub of themes and plugins for Sunbird, the Discord Phoenix unlocker. Pick a theme in one click, add plugins read line by line before they ship, or make your own.

Themes

Sunbird recolors all of Discord from a theme's palette: the backgrounds, the text, the accent, the borders, even how round the corners are. Pick one in Ultracord's settings, under Theme. Windows desktop only for now.

      Install

      1. Patch Discord Phoenix with Sunbird

        Open sunbird.tools on your phone and keep the plugin runtime turned on. Desktop Phoenix works too.

      2. Add Ultracord

        In Discord's settings, open Phoenix (Sunbird on desktop), choose Install from URL (+ URL on desktop) and paste this link:

        https://assets.ultracord.dev/plugins/ultracord.js

      3. Pick a theme and your plugins

        Open Ultracord's page in Sunbird's settings, or its button in the chat header on mobile. Choose a theme under Theme on desktop, then pick plugins: each one shows what it can access, and Ultracord tells you when an update is out.

      Contribute

      Themes are .theme.css palettes and plugins are TypeScript files, both submitted as pull requests on GitHub. A theme has to keep its text readable on its backgrounds. A plugin is refused if it:

      • loads or runs code it does not ship with;
      • sends your data anywhere its description does not mention;
      • acts on your account without you asking;
      • is obfuscated or minified.
